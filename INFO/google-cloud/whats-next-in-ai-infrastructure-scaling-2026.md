## TL;DR

As AI expands to autonomous agents and physical systems, the computing infrastructure powering them must be faster, more dynamic, and highly cost-effective. In this Spotlight session with Mark Lohmeyer, you'll learn how Google’s leading AI infrastructure stack - from custom silicon to open software - is powering the agentic and physical AI era. You will get a firsthand look into Google’s roadmap for next-gen TPU, GPU, and Arm systems, and new storage and networking offerings built for training, RL, and inference. Discover how new software capabilities, including AI Frameworks, GKE, and Vertex AI provide the engineering edge to secure, optimize, and scale your agentic and physical AI initiatives. Plus, you'll see how leading AI labs, digital-native, and enterprise customers leverage Google's full stack to drive transformation in their business.

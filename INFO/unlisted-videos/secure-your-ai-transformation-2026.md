## TL;DR

While threat actors are increasingly operationalizing AI to accelerate reconnaissance and social engineering, the vast majority of successful intrusions still stem from fundamental human and systemic failures. 

Organizations are currently facing a critical juncture: cybersecurity is the #1 concern in AI transformation, yet ""Shadow AI"" and missing IT hygiene often pose a greater risk than novel AI-specific attacks.

This session moves past the hype to deliver actionable strategies for securing your AI journey. We will analyze the expanding attack surface created by the modern AI stack and discuss how to transition from a reactive security posture to one of active validation.

Key Takeaways:

Gain Insights Into AI Threats: We begin with frontline insights into the adversarial operationalization of AI.
Establish AI Governance: Learn why robust governance makes organizations 2x more likely to successfully adopt advanced agentic AI and helps mitigate the risks of ""Shadow AI"".
Operationalize Active Validation: Discover why defenders must transition from static Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) to behavioral Indicators of Activity (IOAs) to detect malware that dynamically changes its code.
Implement AI Red Teaming: Understand how to conduct AI-specific red teaming to identify and measure risks in production, such as model theft, data poisoning, and prompt injection.
Leverage Frontline Expertise: Explore how specialized expertise can help you assess your threat models and training data defenses to ensure secure innovation from build to production."

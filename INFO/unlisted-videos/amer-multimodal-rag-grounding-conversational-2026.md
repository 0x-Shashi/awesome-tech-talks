## TL;DR

Realtime Voice AI is only as powerful as the data it can access. In this intermediate session, we tackle the critical challenge of AI search using Google’s state-of-the-art Gemini Embeddings 2 model. Moving beyond simple text retrieval, we will demonstrate how to build an enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline capable of mapping text, audio transcripts, PDFs, and rich media into a shared vector space. By integrating this advanced semantic search into our hero app, you will learn how to give your agents the ability to instantly query vast amounts of unstructured data to provide highly contextualized answers.

In this session, we will demystify the process of building custom AI agents on Gemini Enterprise. We will walk through how to create an AI agent using two different methods: a quick method using natural language prompts, and a more granular method using the visual agent builder. We will discuss "grounding", which is the process of connecting agents to your company’s data and systems to ensure it has the data and context that is most relevant to your organization.

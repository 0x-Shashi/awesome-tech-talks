## Origins across Silicon Valley hacker culture, French philosophy, and media labs

- **Douglas Edric Stanley**: Immersed in 1970s Silicon Valley tech culture before moving to Paris to study post-structuralist philosophy, subsequently developing interactive art installations at Centre Pompidou and teaching creative coding in Aix-en-Provence.
- **Anthony Masure**: Dean of Research at Geneva University of Art and Design (HEAD Geneve), investigating the critical intersections of AI, algorithmic culture, and design pedagogy.

## Cybernetic loops and the temporality of human-machine interaction

- **Recursive temporality**: Unlike linear traditional media (cinema, novels), digital systems operate on recursive clock cycles.
- **Cybernetic feedback**: Cultural practices shape algorithmic architectures, and algorithmic responses recursively reshape human cognitive patterns and language.

## The role of the designer: holistic context vs. automated optimization

- **Context over statistical average**: While generative models produce generic amalgamations of existing web data, human designers formulate solutions tailored to unique institutional, physical, and relational constraints.
- **Problem discovery**: Design education focuses on reframing underlying questions rather than rapidly generating surface-level aesthetic outputs.

## Resisting late-capitalist maintenance: preserving the craft of drawing

- **Avoiding the button-pusher trap**: When creative production is reduced to single-click model inference, humans become mere caretakers and maintainers of automated machinery.
- **Physical-digital integration**: Partnering with Swiss utensil maker Caran d'Ache, researchers investigated how AI can support creative exploration without destroying the tactile rhythm and joy of physical drawing.

## Hybrid strategies: AI as an experimental medium rather than a turnkey tool

Insights from the exhibition *Assembling Intelligence - Hybrid Strategies for AI, Art, and Design*:

| Artistic Approach | Medium Integration | Aesthetic Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **Retrained Small LLMs** | 3D Sculpting Prompts | AI guides human physical sculpting actions rather than generating 3D meshes |
| **Prompt Poetry** | Text on Gallery Walls | Exposes absurd semantic disconnects between text inputs and visual diffusion outputs |
| **Material Fusion** | Physical Ceramics & Oil | Merges algorithmic generation with tactile clay and traditional glaze techniques |

## Ancient roots of generative text: from the I Ching to non-linear narratives

- **Millennia of generative desire**: From ancient divination mechanisms like the *I Ching* (generating poetic text from randomized yarrow sticks) to modern neural networks, humanity has sought dialogue with non-human intelligences.
- **Adaptive storytelling**: Generative models unlock genuine non-linear literature where narrative worlds evolve continuously around reader actions.
